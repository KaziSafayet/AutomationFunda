## 🔍 How to Do Deep Competitor Research (Research Methodology)

### Step 1: Monitor Their Websites Regularly

**Tool:** [Visualping.io](https://visualping.io) or [Distill.io](https://distill.io)
- Set up alerts on competitor homepages and pricing pages
- Get notified via email when they change content, add new services, or update pricing
- Monitor weekly for top competitors (Autoesta, HireGHLDeveloper, Axe Automation)

---

### Step 2: Analyze Their SEO & Traffic

**Tools:**
- [Ahrefs Free Webmaster Tools](https://ahrefs.com) — See which keywords drive traffic to their site
- [Semrush Free Trial](https://semrush.com) — View their top pages, backlinks, keyword gaps
- [SimilarWeb](https://similarweb.com) — Estimate their monthly traffic and traffic sources

**What to look for:**
- What keywords rank for them that you don't rank for yet?
- Which blog posts get the most traffic? (Replicate + improve them)
- Where does their traffic come from? (LinkedIn, Google, referrals?)

---

### Step 3: Follow Their Social Media & LinkedIn

**Action:** Follow the founder accounts of Autoesta, HireGHLDeveloper, and Axe Automation on LinkedIn.

**What to study:**
- What content formats do they post? (carousels, text posts, videos)
- Which posts get the most engagement? (likes, comments, shares)
- What pain points do they talk about?
- Are they running LinkedIn ads? (check their LinkedIn Page → "Posts" → filter "Ads")

**Tool:** [Mindsoup](https://mindsoup.io) or [Shield Analytics](https://shieldapp.ai) — Track competitor LinkedIn performance

---

### Step 4: Read Their Customer Reviews

**Platforms to check:**
- **Clutch.co** — Most authoritative for agency reviews. Search each competitor name
- **G2.com** — For any SaaS-adjacent tools they've built
- **Google Business Reviews** — Check their Google Business listing
- **Upwork Profile** — If they have one, read client feedback word-by-word

**What to extract:**
- What do clients praise? (service quality, communication, results)
- What do clients complain about? (missed deadlines, bad handover, communication gaps)
- Use their weaknesses in your own marketing messaging

---

### Step 5: Reverse-Engineer Their Offer & Pricing

**Method:**
1. Submit an inquiry on their site and see their proposal process
2. Note their response time, quality of initial communication, and what questions they ask
3. Ask for a quote and see how they structure pricing
4. Compare their deliverables to yours

**This gives you:**
- Their true pricing (not just "contact us")
- Their proposal template
- Their sales sequence (how many follow-ups, what content they send)

---

### Step 6: Track Their Content Marketing

**RSS Monitoring:**
- Add competitor blogs to [Feedly](https://feedly.com) to get notified of new posts
- Check their blog monthly for new topics

**YouTube / Video:**
- Search their brand names on YouTube — do they have tutorial content?
- If they do, note what topics they cover and what's missing

**Newsletter:**
- Subscribe to any email newsletters they send
- Study their nurture sequences, subject lines, and offers

---

### Step 7: Track LinkedIn Job Postings

When a competitor posts jobs, it reveals:
- **"We're hiring a GoHighLevel specialist"** → they're scaling GHL work
- **"Looking for an n8n developer"** → they're entering your space
- **"We need a sales manager"** → they're investing in growth

**Tool:** Set up a LinkedIn job alert with competitor company names.

---

### Step 8: Monitor Their Backlink Profile

**Tool:** Ahrefs or Moz Free → Backlink checker

- Who is linking to them? (directories, review sites, partner blogs)
- Get listed in the same directories
- Reach out to the same blogs for guest posts

---

### Step 9: Check Their Template / Product Offerings

If competitors sell templates, products, or courses:
- Buy their cheapest product — study the quality and positioning
- Compare to your own templates
- Note gaps you can fill with better quality or lower price

---

### Step 10: Build a Competitor Intelligence Spreadsheet

Create a Google Sheet with the following columns, updated quarterly:

| Column | What to Track |
|--------|---------------|
| Company | Name + website |
| Services | What they offer |
| Pricing | Known price points |
| ICP | Who they target |
| Top Keywords | From Ahrefs/Semrush |
| Monthly Traffic | From SimilarWeb |
| LinkedIn Followers | Company page |
| Clutch Rating | Score + number of reviews |
| Last Checked | Date of last review |
| Notes | Any changes or observations |
